## Risk Assessment
- **Phase 1 is large (29 h after the red team) and on the critical path:** steps 1–4 are the gate for group B; if late, the parent may start phases 10 and 11 (web) after step 6.
- **Changing the JWT claims:** old access tokens are refused once; the RT7 backfill gives every live family a `user_sessions` row, so the web's refresh path silently re-issues tokens with the new claims (verified in step 4); nobody is logged out.
- **`org_session` is on every request:** `check_org_access` must stay one indexed read (the policy row is fetched with the membership query, joined); measure p95 before and after on the auth smoke.
- **The RT1 rewrite touches six M5–M7 service files:** mechanical (`ROLE_PERMISSIONS[ctx.role]` → `ctx.permissions`), behaviour-neutral for sessions, their suites re-run in step 4.
- **`ALTER TYPE … ADD VALUE` in the same migration as tables that use the new values:** none use them in DDL; inserts happen after commit.
